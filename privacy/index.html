PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Short version: forms you fill reach the site owner by email. Nothing is sold, nothing is shared, analytics are minimal.

What is collected

If you use the contact form, you share your name, email address, and message. If you join the newsletter or a notify-me list, you share your email address. Server logs (IP address, pages visited, timestamps) are kept briefly by the hosting provider for security and diagnostics.

How it is used

Contact messages are emailed to the site owner to reply to you. Newsletter addresses receive the occasional update you signed up for — roughly one email a month, and every email explains how to unsubscribe. Nothing is sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing.

Cookies and advertising

The site itself sets no tracking cookies. If display advertising is enabled in the future (e.g. Google AdSense), the ad provider may use cookies to serve and measure ads — see Google's advertising privacy policy for details. You can block cookies in your browser; the site keeps working.

Your rights

Write to mrxubr@gmail.com to ask what is held about you, correct it, or delete it — including unsubscribing from all lists. Requests are answered within a reasonable time.
