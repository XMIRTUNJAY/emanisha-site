    What you will learn

    Twenty Marathi words for how you feel — from आनंद (happiness) and दुःख (sorrow) to the useful in-between states: कंटाळा (boredom), थकवा (tiredness), काळजी (worry). Several entries here are body-states too — भूक (hunger), तहान (thirst), झोप (sleepiness) — because Marathi, like Hindi, talks about them as things that happen to you.

    That grammar matters as much as the words: feelings and body-states usually take the मला … आहे pattern (literally “to me is…”), which is why this page pairs with Unit 17 and the emotions lessons where sentences like मला थोडं दुःख आहे appear.

    Tip: Learn feelings in opposite pairs: आनंद/दुःख, भीती/धैर्य, कंटाळा/उत्साह — pairs stick better than lists.

    The मला … आहे pattern

    Many Marathi feeling sentences put the feeling as the subject and the person in the dative: मला राग आला (I got angry), मला भीती वाटते (I feel afraid), मला भूक लागली आहे (I am hungry). Translating word-for-word from English (“I am hungry”) produces Marathi nobody says.

    Unit 17 drills exactly this: मी आज खूप आनंदी आहे for states you are, and मला … for states that come over you.

    For Hindi speakers: राग is a trap

    In Hindi, राग is a musical mode. In Marathi, राग means anger. The word is identical, the meaning is not — and it is one of the highest-frequency feeling words in Marathi. Same story, smaller stakes, with हसू (laughter, a noun — Hindi speakers expect हँसना).

    Common mistakes

    ✗ मी भूक आहे (I am hunger)→✓ मला भूक लागली आहे

    Hunger, thirst and fear arrive “to you” in Marathi — the dative pattern, like Hindi मुझे भूख लगी है.

    ✗ Using राग to talk about music→✓ राग = anger in Marathi

    False friend: Hindi राग (melody) ≠ Marathi राग (anger).

    ✗ दुख for दुःख→✓ दुःख (duḥkh) with the visarga

    Standard Marathi keeps the ः; writing दुख is the Hindi spelling habit.

    Quick practice

    Answer first, then open the reveal to check yourself.

    1. How do you say “anger” in Marathi?
    1. भीती
    2. राग
    3. दुःख
    4. लाज

    Answer: B. राग — राग (rāg) = anger — not music, as in Hindi.

    2. भीती means…
    1. Love
    2. Pride
    3. Fear
    4. Surprise

    Answer: C. Fear — भीती (bhītī) = fear; it takes the dative: मला भीती वाटते.

    3. Which word means “worry”?
    1. काळजी
    2. उत्साह
    3. अभिमान
    4. तहान

    Answer: A. काळजी — काळजी (kāḷjī) = worry; it can also mean “care” in care-taking contexts.

    4. Translate to Marathi: “I am very happy today.”

    मी आज खूप आनंदी आहे. — From Unit 17 — आनंदी (happy) is the adjective form of आनंद.

    Common questions

    How do you say “I am sad” in Marathi?

    मला दुःख आहे or मी दुःखी आहे — the curriculum sentence is मला थोडं दुःख आहे (I feel a little sad), using the dative मला pattern natural for feelings.

    What is the Marathi word for love?

    प्रेम (prem) — same as Hindi प्यार’s formal cousin. Family affection appears in sentences like आईला खूप प्रेम आहे.
