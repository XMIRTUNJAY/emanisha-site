    What you will learn

    Thirty-six Marathi abstract words from the Abstract category: जग (world), देश (country), भाषा (language), धर्म (religion), भविष्य (future), भूतकाळ (past), परिणाम (result), मूल्य (value), गुणवत्ता (quality), धैर्य (patience/courage), अनुभव (experience), उद्देश (purpose), समस्या (problem), उपाय (solution), परिवर्तन (change). Every entry has Hindi and English meanings, pronunciation and a philosophical example sentence.

    Many abstract words are shared Sanskrit loans (देश, धर्म, भाषा, इतिहास, भविष्य, संस्कृती) - identical to Hindi. The shifts are in the functional words: जग (world), परिणाम (result), उपाय (solution), धैर्य (patience). These are the words that elevate conversation from transactional to meaningful.

    Tip: Learn in semantic clusters: time (भविष्य, भूतकाळ, वर्तमान), value (मूल्य, गुणवत्ता, परिणाम), agency (उद्देश, उपाय, धैर्य, अनुभव). The Sanskrit loans are free; the Marathi-specific ones are the leverage.

    Time & existence

    भविष्य (future), भूतकाळ (past), वर्तमान (present), जग (world), अस्तित्व (existence). The triad भविष्य/भूतकाळ/वर्तमान mirrors Hindi exactly — free vocabulary.

    Values & agency

    मूल्य (value), गुणवत्ता (quality), परिणाम (result), उद्देश (purpose), उपाय (solution), धैर्य (patience/courage), अनुभव (experience), परिवर्तन (change). These are the words of serious conversation — decisions, planning, reflection.

    Common mistakes

    ✗ Using दुनिया for world→✓ जग = world (Marathi); दुनिया is Hindi/Urdu

    दुनिया is the Hindi/Urdu word; जग (jag) is the standard Marathi word for world.

    ✗ Using भूतकाल for past→✓ भूतकाळ = past (Marathi spelling)

    Hindi भूतकाल vs Marathi भूतकाळ — the ला/काळ shift is systematic.

    ✗ Using समस्या for all problems→✓ समस्या = problem; अडचण = difficulty/obstacle; त्रास = trouble/distress

    Marathi distinguishes problem types: समस्या (formal problem), अडचण (practical difficulty), त्रास (distress).

    Quick practice

    Answer first, then open the reveal to check yourself.

    1. World in Marathi?
    1. दुनिया
    2. जग
    3. संसार
    4. पृथ्वी

    Answer: B. जग — जग (jag) — the standard Marathi word for world.

    2. Future in Marathi?
    1. भविष्य
    2. अन्य
    3. विकल्प
    4. चौथा

    Answer: A. भविष्य — भविष्य (bhaviṣya) — identical to Hindi, free vocabulary.

    3. Patience/courage in Marathi?
    1. सहनशीलता
    2. धैर्य
    3. साहस
    4. सहनशक्ति

    Answer: B. धैर्य — धैर्य (dhairya) = patience/courage — covers both nuances.

    4. Translate to Marathi: “Time changes everything.”

    वेळ सर्व काहीच बदलते. — वेळ (time) + सर्व (all) + काहीच (anything) + बदलते (changes).
