    What you will learn

    Forty-three Marathi words covering the body head to toe — डोकं (head) down to टाच (heel) — plus the health vocabulary that makes them useful: दुखणं (pain), ताप (fever), औषध (medicine), रुग्ण (patient). Every entry has Hindi and English meanings, pronunciation and an example sentence.

    This list earns its keep at the doctor: Unit 10 sentences like माझं डोके दुखते (my head hurts) are built from these words, so the table below doubles as symptom-reporting vocabulary. Learn the body part together with the pattern, and you can describe what hurts without grammar overhead.

    Tip: Point and name while washing up or dressing — हात, पाय, डोकं three times a day beats one long study session.

    The “it hurts” pattern

    Marathi says माझं डोके दुखते — literally “my head hurts”, with the body part as the subject. So the sentence frame you need is माझं/माझे + body part + दुखते. The same frame extends to symptoms: मला ताप आहे (I have fever), मला खोकला आहे (I have a cough).

    For Hindi speakers this is familiar territory: मेरा सिर दुख रहा है maps closely. The Marathi-specific part is which words changed — डोकं for सिर, पोट for पेट, खोकला for खाँसी.

    Two-for-one words

    पाय covers both leg and foot; बोट covers both finger and toe; हात covers hand and arm. Context disambiguates — and plural बोटं, पाय appear constantly in everyday speech.

    Common mistakes

    ✗ माझ्याकडे ताप आहे for fever→✓ मला ताप आहे

    Symptoms take the dative मला pattern in the curriculum (मला ताप आहे, खांसीही आहे — Unit 10).

    ✗ Translating “I have a headache” word for word→✓ डोकेदुखी आहे मला, or माझं डोके दुखते

    The thing hurts; you don’t “have” the pain — Marathi prefers the body part as subject.

    ✗ Using औषध and गोळी interchangeably→✓ औषध = medicine; गोळी = pill/tablet

    औषध जेवणानंतर घ्या (take medicine after food) vs. counting गोळया.

    Quick practice

    Answer first, then open the reveal to check yourself.

    1. How do you say “eye” in Marathi?
    1. नाक
    2. डोळा
    3. कान
    4. तोंड

    Answer: B. डोळा — डोळा (ḍoḷā) — one of several fully Marathi words where Hindi uses आँख.

    2. पोट means…
    1. Back
    2. Chest
    3. Stomach
    4. Waist

    Answer: C. Stomach — पोट (poṭ) = stomach — hence डॉक्टर, माझं पोट दुखते.

    3. What is औषध?
    1. Exercise
    2. Health
    3. Medicine
    4. Fever

    Answer: C. Medicine — औषध (auṣadh) = medicine; आरोग्य is health.

    4. Translate to Marathi: “Doctor, my stomach hurts.”

    डॉक्टर, माझं पोट दुखते. — Straight from Unit 10 — body part as the subject.
