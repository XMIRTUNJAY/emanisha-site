    What you will learn

    A curated selection of Marathi personality adjectives from the Adjectives (68) and Abstract (36) categories: स्वभाव (nature/temperament), मन (mind/heart), बुद्धी (intelligence), धीर (patient), उत्साही (enthusiastic), संवेदनशील (sensitive), दृढ (determined), लाजाळू (shy). Every entry has Hindi and English meanings, pronunciation, gender agreement notes and example sentences.

    Personality words follow the same gender agreement as all adjectives: masculine ends in -ा, feminine in -ी, neuter in -े. Learn the citation form (masculine) and the pattern gives you all three.

    Tip: Learn in opposite pairs: धीर/उतावळा (patient/impatient), उत्साही/उदासीन (enthusiastic/apathetic), संवेदनशील/कठोर (sensitive/hard-hearted).

    Core personality traits

    Positive: धीर (patient), उत्साही (enthusiastic), दृढ (determined), संवेदनशील (sensitive), बुद्धिमान (intelligent), सरळ (simple/honest), उदार (generous). Negative: उतावळा (impatient), उदासीन (apathetic), कठोर (hard/stern), स्वार्थी (selfish), अहंकारी (arrogant). Each agrees with the person described.

    Gender agreement in action

    तो धीर आहे (he is patient), ती धीर आहे (she is patient), ते धीर आहे (it/child is patient). The adjective ending changes: धीर (masc), धीर (fem - same), धीर (neut - same). For adjectives ending in -ा: उत्साही (masc), उत्साही (fem), उत्साही (neut). The pattern is consistent.

    Common mistakes

    ✗ Using स्वभाव as an adjective→✓ स्वभाव = nature/temperament (noun); use धीर, उत्साही for adjectives

    स्वभाव is the noun "temperament"; to describe someone, use the adjectives that derive from it.

    Quick practice

    Answer first, then open the reveal to check yourself.

    1. Patient in Marathi?
    1. उतावळा
    2. धीर
    3. उत्साही
    4. सरळ

    Answer: B. धीर — धीर (dhīr) = patient; opposite is उतावळा (impatient).

    2. Shy in Marathi?
    1. लाजाळू
    2. सरळ
    3. उत्साही
    4. धीर

    Answer: A. लाजाळू — लाजाळू (lājāḷū) = shy/bashful.

    3. Intelligent in Marathi?
    1. बुद्धी
    2. बुद्धिमान
    3. मन
    4. स्वभाव

    Answer: B. बुद्धिमान — बुद्धिमान (buddhīmān) = intelligent; बुद्धी = intelligence (noun).

    4. Translate to Marathi: “She is very patient.”

    ती खूप धीर आहे. — ती (she) + धीर (patient) + आहे.

    Common questions

    How many personality words are on this page?

    A curated selection of ~25 personality adjectives from the Adjectives and Abstract categories, with gender agreement and examples.

    How do personality adjectives agree with gender?

    Same as all adjectives: masculine -ा, feminine -ी, neuter -े. Many personality words (धीर, उत्साही, संवेदनशील) are invariant or follow the -ी declension.
