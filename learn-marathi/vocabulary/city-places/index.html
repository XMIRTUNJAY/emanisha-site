    What you will learn

    Twenty-five Marathi city place words from the Travel category: station (स्टेशन), market (बाजार), hospital (दवाखाना), bank (बँक), temple (मंदिर), bridge (पूल), highway (महामार्ग), airport (विमानतळ). Every entry has Hindi and English meanings, pronunciation and a real travel example sentence.

    Units 14 and 15 sentences like मुंबईला जाणारा ट्रेन कोणता? and ऑटो घेऊन स्टेशनला गेले put these words in the exact exchanges you will have navigating a Maharashtrian city.

    Tip: Learn in functional groups: transport hubs (स्टेशन, प्लॅटफॉर्म, बस स्टॉप), services (दवाखाना, बँक, पोस्ट ऑफिस), landmarks (मंदिर, मस्जिद, गुरुद्वारा, चर्च).

    Transport hubs

    स्टेशन (station), प्लॅटफॉर्म (platform), तिकीट (ticket), तिकीटघर (ticket office), बस स्टॉप (bus stop), विमानतळ (airport), बंदर (port/harbor). These get you from A to B.

    Services & landmarks

    दवाखाना (clinic/hospital), बँक (bank), मंदिर (temple), मस्जिद (mosque), गुरुद्वारा (gurudwara), चर्च (church), पोस्ट ऑफिस (post office), पुलिस स्टेशन (police station). The words you need when something goes wrong or you need a service.

    Common mistakes

    ✗ Using अस्पताल for hospital→✓ दवाखाना = hospital (Marathi)

    अस्पताल is Hindi/Urdu; दवाखाना (drug-house) is the standard Marathi word on signs and in speech.

    ✗ Saying पुल for bridge→✓ पूल = bridge

    पुल is Hindi; पूल is the Marathi spelling convention for the English loan.

    ✗ Confusing बाजार and मार्केट→✓ बाजार = traditional market; मार्केट = modern market/mall

    Both exist; बाजार is the everyday word for a street market, मार्केट for organized markets.

    Quick practice

    Answer first, then open the reveal to check yourself.

    1. Hospital in Marathi?
    1. अस्पताल
    2. दवाखाना
    3. हॉस्पिटल
    4. चिकित्सालय

    Answer: B. दवाखाना — दवाखाना (davākhānā) — the standard Marathi word on signs and in daily speech.

    2. Bridge in Marathi?
    1. पुल
    2. पूल
    3. सेतू
    4. ब्रिज

    Answer: B. पूल — पूल (pūl) — Marathi spelling of the English loan.

    3. Airport in Marathi?
    1. विमानतळ
    2. एयरपोर्ट
    3. हवाई अड्डा
    4. उड्डयन केंद्र

    Answer: A. विमानतळ — विमानतळ (vimāntaḷ) — विमान (plane) + तल (ground).
