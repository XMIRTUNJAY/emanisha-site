    What you will learn

    Thirty Marathi adverbs for how often (नेहमी, कधीच, कधीकधी), when (आता, लवकर, उशिरा), how (हळूहळू, जोरात, नीट, चुकून, मुद्दाम) and degree (फक्त, खरंच, नक्की, कदाचित, जवळजवळ). Every entry has Hindi and English meanings, pronunciation and a real example sentence.

    Unit 18 places adverbs right before the verb: मी रोज क्रिकेट खेळतो, मला कधीकधी वाचन आवडतं. The pattern is consistent — learn the position once, apply to all.

    Tip: नेहमी (always) vs कधीच (never) — one letter च flips the meaning. कधी = when; कधी+च = never.

    Frequency ladder

    नेहमी (always) → कधीकधी (sometimes) → कधीच (never). Add रोज (daily) and अजून (still) for the full daily spectrum.

    Directional adverbs from inside/outside

    बाहेरून (from outside), आतून (from inside), वरून (from above), खालून (from below), समोरून (from the front) — spatial adverbs built from direction words + the -ऊन suffix.

    Common mistakes

    ✗ Using अजून for “yet” in negative sentences→✓ अजून = still; अजूनही = yet / still (emphatic)

    The emphatic ही adds the “up to now” nuance Hindi speakers expect from अभी तक.

    ✗ Saying कधी for never→✓ कधी = when; कधीच = never

    The च particle negates — a tiny suffix, a total meaning flip.

    ✗ Placing adverb after the verb→✓ Adverb sits before the verb: मी रोज खेळतो (not मी खेळतो रोज)

    Unit 18 pattern — adverbs pre-verb, consistently.

    Quick practice

    Answer first, then open the reveal to check yourself.

    1. नेहमी means…
    1. Sometimes
    2. Always
    3. Never
    4. Often

    Answer: B. Always — नेहमी (nehmi) = always.

    2. कधीच means…
    1. Always
    2. Sometimes
    3. Never
    4. Often

    Answer: C. Never — कधीच (kadhīc) = never (कधी + च).

    3. Which adverb means “slowly”?
    1. हळूहळू
    2. लगेच
    3. जोरात
    4. नीट

    Answer: A. हळूहळू — हळूहळू (haḷūhaḷū) — reduplicated, like Hindi धीरे-धीरे.

    4. Translate to Marathi: “I play cricket daily.”

    Common questions

    How many adverbs are on this page?

    30 — frequency, time, manner, degree and directional adverbs, each with Hindi meaning and sentence context.

    Where do adverbs go in a Marathi sentence?

    Before the verb — मी रोज खेळतो, मला कधीकधी आवडतं. Unit 18 drills this position consistently.
