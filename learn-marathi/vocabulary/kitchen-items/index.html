    What you will learn

    Forty Marathi kitchen words covering rooms (स्वयंपाकघर, भोजनकक्ष), utensils (कढाई, तवा, चमचा), appliances (मिक्सर, फ्रिज, स्टोव), and cooking verbs (उकळणे, तळणे, भाजणे). Every entry has Hindi and English meanings, pronunciation and a real example sentence.

    Unit 03 introduces the kitchen as a room (स्वयंपाकघर) and the Food category adds ingredients and cooking verbs. Together they form a complete kitchen vocabulary for daily use and recipes.

    Tip: Pair nouns with cooking verbs: कढाई + तळणे (fry in a wok), तवा + भाजणे (roast on a griddle), पतीला + उकळणे (boil in a pot).

    Common mistakes

    ✗ Using किचन for kitchen→✓ स्वयंपाकघर = kitchen (Marathi)

    किचन is an English loan; स्वयंपाकघर is the standard Marathi word used in daily speech and real estate.

    ✗ Using किचन for kitchen→✓ स्वयंपाकघर = kitchen (Marathi)

    किचन is an English loan; स्वयंपाकघर is the standard Marathi word used in daily speech and real estate.

    ✗ Saying चमचा for all spoons→✓ चमचा = spoon; चलणी = ladle; चलणी = strainer

    Different utensils have different names. चमचा is specifically a spoon.

    1. Kitchen in Marathi?
    1. किचन

    2. What is "fridge" in Marathi?
    1. फ्रिज
    2. फ्रीजर
    3. कूलर
    4. आइस बॉक्स

    Answer: A. फ्रिज — फ्रिज (phrij) — the standard Marathi word for refrigerator.

    3. "Roast on a griddle" in Marathi?
    1. तवा भाजा
    2. कढाई भाजा

    4. ओव्हन भाजा

    4. Translate to Marathi: “Fry the onions in a wok.”

    सामग्री कटोरीत मिश्रण करा. — कटोरीत = in the bowl (locative); मिश्रण करा = mix (imperative).
