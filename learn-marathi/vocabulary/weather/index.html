    What you will learn

    Sixteen Marathi words for talking about the sky: the four seasons (उन्हाळा, पावसाळा, हिवाळा, वसंत), rain, sunshine, wind and clouds, plus useful everyday words like छत्री (umbrella) and हवामान (weather itself). Each entry has Hindi and English meanings, pronunciation and an example sentence.

    Weather is the safest small-talk topic in any language, and Marathi is no exception. A handful of these words — पाऊस आला, खूप ऊन आहे, वारा आहे — carry a whole conversation with a neighbour, a rickshaw driver or a colleague.

    Tip: Anchor the four seasons to the Maharashtra calendar: पावसाळा (monsoon) arrives in June — the season Maharashtra talks about most.

    The four seasons

    उन्हाळा (summer), पावसाळा (monsoon), हिवाळा (winter), वसंत (spring). Note the -आळा ending on three of them — once you hear the pattern, the season words stop needing memorization.

    Monsoon matters in Maharashtra: curriculum sentences like वर्षात पावस खूप पडतो (it rains a lot in the rainy season) from Unit 16 are the kind of line you will actually hear.

    Hindi speakers: watch out for हवा

    In Hindi, हवा usually means wind. In Marathi, wind is वारा — and हवा means air. The false friend is easy to trip on precisely because the word is familiar; the table below marks wind clearly as वारा.

    Common mistakes

    ✗ Saying हवा for wind→✓ वारा = wind; Marathi हवा = air

    The most common false friend on this page for Hindi speakers.

    ✗ Using उन्हाळा to say “it is hot”→✓ उन्हाळा = the summer season; heat itself is उष्णता, and sunshine is ऊन

    Season name vs. weather condition — different words.

    ✗ थंडी for a cold (illness)→✓ थंडी = cold weather; a cold (illness) is सर्दी

    Hindi merges these; Marathi separates the weather from the sickness.

    Quick practice

    Answer first, then open the reveal to check yourself.

    1. How do you say “rain” in Marathi?
    1. वारा
    2. ढग
    3. पाऊस
    4. ऊन

    Answer: C. पाऊस — पाऊस (pāūs) = rain; the season is पावसाळा.

    2. हिवाळा means…
    1. Summer
    2. Monsoon
    3. Spring
    4. Winter

    Answer: D. Winter — हिवाळा (hivāḷā) = winter.

    3. What is धुकं?
    1. Fog
    2. Storm
    3. Lightning
    4. Cloud

    Answer: A. Fog — धुकं (dhuka) = fog; a storm is वादळ and lightning is वीज.

    4. Fill in the blank: पावसाळ्यात खूप ___ पडतो.

    पाऊस — Patterned on Unit 16: वर्षात पावस खूप पडतो.

    Common questions

    What are the four seasons in Marathi?

    उन्हाळा (summer), पावसाळा (monsoon), हिवाळा (winter) and वसंत (spring) — three end in -आळा, which makes them easy to remember as a set.

    How do you say “weather” in Marathi?

    हवामान (havāmān). Rain is पाऊस, wind is वारा, and sunshine is ऊन.
