    What you will learn

    Thirty-eight Marathi clothing words in two layers: everyday and modern wear (शर्ट, पँट, जीन्स, चप्पल) and traditional Maharashtrian clothing and jewellery (साडी, धोतर, पगडी, नथ, बांगड्या). Each entry carries Hindi and English meanings, pronunciation and an example sentence.

    Notice how freely Marathi absorbs loanwords: शर्ट, जाकीट, रेनकोट, टीशर्ट. That is real, everyday Marathi — you do not need to hunt for “pure” words nobody uses. The traditional half of the list is where genuinely Marathi vocabulary lives.

    Tip: Pair this list with the shopping pages — clothing words become useful the moment you can ask हे किती आहे? (how much is this?).

    Traditional Maharashtrian wear

    धोतर, पगडी, सदरा and ओढणी are words you will hear at festivals, weddings and in older parts of any Maharashtrian city. नववारी साडी पाहिली असेल तर — the nine-yard sari — साडी is the everyday word.

    Jewellery has its own mini-cluster: कानातले (earrings), नथ (nose ring), बांगड्या (bangles), अंगठी (ring). These are high-frequency words in festival and wedding conversation.

    For Hindi speakers

    Most clothing words carry over cleanly (कुर्ता, पायजामा, दुपट्टा are shared), but watch जोडा: in Marathi it means a pair of footwear specifically, not a generic pair. And पँट is the everyday word for trousers.

    Common mistakes

    ✗ Using जोडा for any pair→✓ जोडा = a pair of footwear; for other pairs use जोडी

    Narrower than Hindi जोड़ा.

    ✗ Mixing टोपी and पगडी→✓ टोपी = cap; पगडी = turban

    Both go on the head; only पगडी is the traditional wrapped turban.

    ✗ Hunting for “pure Marathi” for शर्ट or जीन्स→✓ Loanwords are standard Marathi here

    शर्ट, जीन्स, टीशर्ट are the words people actually say.

    Quick practice

    Answer first, then open the reveal to check yourself.

    1. How do you say “sari” in Marathi?
    1. ओढणी
    2. साडी
    3. धोतर
    4. लेंगा

    Answer: B. साडी — साडी (sāḍī) — same word, same garment.

    2. चप्पल means…
    1. Cap
    2. Belt
    3. Sandals / slippers
    4. Socks

    Answer: C. Sandals / slippers — चप्पल (chappal). Boots for rain are गमबूट.

    3. What are बांगड्या?
    1. Earrings
    2. Bangles
    3. Necklaces
    4. Rings

    Answer: B. Bangles — बांगड्या (bāṅgaḍyā) = bangles.

    4. Fill in the blank: मराठीत “cap” ला ___ म्हणतात.

    टोपी — टोपी = cap; पगडी = turban — different words.

    Common questions

    How do you say “clothes” in Marathi?

    कपडे (kapaḍe). Individual items: शर्ट (shirt), पँट (trousers), साडी (sari), चप्पल (sandals) — 38 words in the table above.

    Which Marathi clothing words are unique to Maharashtra?

    धोतर, पगडी, नऊवारी-style साडी vocabulary, सदरा and ओढणी are the traditional words; modern items like शर्ट and जीन्स are loanwords used as-is.
