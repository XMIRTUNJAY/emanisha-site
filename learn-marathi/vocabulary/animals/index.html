    What you will learn

    Forty Marathi animal names from the Bol Marathi curriculum: pets like कुत्रा and मांजर, farm animals like गाय and म्हैस, wildlife like वाघ and हत्ती, birds from कावळा to मोर, and even the small ones — मुंगी (ant) and फुलपाखरू (butterfly). Every entry carries its Hindi meaning, English meaning, pronunciation and a real example sentence.

    Hindi speakers get a head start here, but not a free pass: गाय is identical, घोडा is near-identical, yet a cat is मांजर, not बिल्ली, and a dog softens to कुत्रा. The familiar-looking words and the genuinely new ones are mixed together in the table below — check each against the Hindi column instead of assuming.

    Tip: Learn in habitat groups: house (कुत्रा, मांजर), farm (गाय, म्हैस, बकरी), jungle (वाघ, सिंह, हत्ती), sky (पोपट, कावळा, गरुड).

    For Hindi speakers: familiar, but not identical

    Several Marathi animal words are Hindi words with a sound shift: कुत्ता → कुत्रा, हाथी → हत्ती, खरगोश → ससा is a full replacement, and चूहा → उंदीर. The pattern to trust: if the Marathi word looks like a softened Hindi word, it usually means the same thing (कुत्रा, हत्ती, घोडा). If it looks unrelated (ससा, म्हैस, मांजर), learn it fresh.

    Marathi also marks gender explicitly in animal pairs: घोडा (horse) / घोडी (mare), and हरण (deer) beside हरीण (stag). The table below shows both where both exist.

    Common mistakes

    ✗ Calling a tiger सिंह→✓ वाघ = tiger; सिंह = lion

    Hindi बाघ (tiger) and शेर (lion) look nothing like the Marathi pair, so learners mix them up. वाघ is tiger.

    ✗ पोपट treated as any bird→✓ पोपट = parrot; पक्षी = bird (generic)

    पक्षी is the category word — the word for the whole group, not one species.

    ✗ Writing घोडी for a male horse→✓ घोडा = horse, घोडी = mare

    Marathi keeps an explicit feminine form here; Hindi घोड़ी exists too but English 'horse' hides the gender.

    Quick practice

    Answer first, then open the reveal to check yourself.

    1. How do you say “dog” in Marathi?
    1. मांजर
    2. कुत्रा
    3. गाय
    4. म्हैस

    Answer: B. कुत्रा — कुत्रा (kutrā) — a softened cousin of Hindi कुत्ता.

    2. What does वाघ mean?
    1. Lion
    2. Elephant
    3. Tiger
    4. Wolf

    Answer: C. Tiger — वाघ (vāgh) = tiger. Lion is सिंह.

    3. फुलपाखरू means…
    1. Honeybee
    2. Butterfly
    3. Sparrow
    4. Mosquito

    Answer: B. Butterfly — फुलपाखरू (phulpākharū) = butterfly; मधमाशी is the honeybee.

    4. Fill in the blank: मराठीत “बिल्ली” ला ___ म्हणतात.

    मांजर — Not a sound shift — a different word entirely.

    Common questions

    How many animal words are on this page?

    40 — pets, farm animals, wildlife, birds and insects, each with Hindi and English meanings, pronunciation and an example sentence.

    Is Marathi animal vocabulary the same as Hindi?

    Partly. Words like गाय and घोडा carry over; कुत्रा and हत्ती are sound-shifted; मांजर (cat), ससा (rabbit) and म्हैस (buffalo) are different words you must learn fresh.
