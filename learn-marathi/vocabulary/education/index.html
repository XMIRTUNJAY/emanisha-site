    What you will learn

    Thirty-eight Marathi words for school and education life: classroom (वर्ग, तुकडी), exams (परीक्षा, गुण), subjects (गणित, विज्ञान, भूगोल), degrees (पदवी, गुणपत्रक) and the everyday tools (वही, पेन्सिल, टिफिन). Every entry has Hindi and English meanings, pronunciation and a real example sentence.

    Unit 11 anchors this vocabulary in real situations: interviews, projects, promotions — the curriculum sentences like मी इंजिनिअरिंग शिकत आहे and मला प्रमोशन मिळालं show these words in action.

    Tip: Group by the student journey: admission (प्रवेश, शुल्क) → study (अभ्यास, धडा, विषय) → exam (परीक्षा, उत्तर) → result (गुण, गुणपत्रक, पदवी).

    The exam pipeline

    प्रवेश (admission) → शुल्क (fees) → अभ्यासक्रम (syllabus) → परीक्षा (exam) → गुण (marks) → गुणपत्रक (report card) → पदवी (degree). Each word is a milestone; the sentences in Unit 11 walk you through the whole sequence.

    Subjects cluster

    गणित (mathematics), विज्ञान (science), भूगोल (geography), नागरिकशास्त्र (civics), शारीरिक शिक्षण (physical education) — the -शास्त्र ending marks formal subjects, making them easy to recognize and extend.

    Common mistakes

    ✗ Using उत्तर only for “answer”→✓ उत्तर = answer AND north (direction)

    Same word, two unrelated meanings — context tells you which. The direction word appears in the Directions category (v-dir-04).

    ✗ Calling a report card गुण→✓ गुण = marks; गुणपत्रक = report card

    गुण alone is the score; गुणपत्रक is the document. Hindi अंकपत्र follows the same pattern.

    ✗ Writing परीक्षा for a quiz→✓ परीक्षा = formal exam; small tests are लहान परीक्षा or टेस्ट

    परीक्षा carries weight; casual quizzes borrow टेस्ट.

    Quick practice

    Answer first, then open the reveal to check yourself.

    1. What is “library” in Marathi?
    1. ग्रंथालय
    2. वही
    3. पेन्सिल
    4. गुणपत्रक

    Answer: A. ग्रंथालय — ग्रंथालय (granthālay) — literally “book-place”.

    2. गुणपत्रक means…
    1. Syllabus
    2. Report card
    3. Scholarship
    4. Admission

    Answer: B. Report card — गुणपत्रक (guṇpatrak) = marks-sheet / report card.

    3. Which word means “scholarship”?
    1. शिष्यवृत्ती
    2. शुल्क
    3. प्रवेश
    4. अभ्यासक्रम

    Answer: A. शिष्यवृत्ती — शिष्यवृत्ती (śiṣyavṛttī) — student + growth.

    4. Translate to Marathi: “I am studying engineering.”

    मी इंजिनिअरिंग शिकत आहे. — Unit 11 sentence — present continuous for ongoing study.
