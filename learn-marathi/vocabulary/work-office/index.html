    What you will learn

    Forty-two Marathi work and office words spanning modern roles (अभियंता, वकील, संगणक) and traditional occupations (सुतार, लोहार, सोनार, शिंपी, न्हावी). Every entry has Hindi and English meanings, pronunciation and a real example sentence.

    Many Marathi surnames come from these occupations — सुतार, लोहार, शिंपी, धोबी are both job titles and family names. The list also covers the modern office: पगार (salary), बैठक (meeting), सुट्टी (leave), बिल (bill). Unit 19 sentences like माझा धंदा चांगला चालत आहे put them in context.

    Tip: The occupation words double as surnames — knowing सुतार means “carpenter” helps you recognize the surname Sutār in introductions.

    Traditional occupations — surnames you will meet

    सुतार (carpenter), लोहार (blacksmith), सोनार (goldsmith), शिंपी (tailor), न्हावी (barber), धोबी (washerman), माळी (gardener), गवळी (milkman) — these are living surnames across Maharashtra. The vocabulary row and the family name are identical.

    Modern office essentials

    नोकरी (job), पगार (salary), बैठक (meeting), सुट्टी (leave), संगणक (computer), बिल (bill), रोख (cash), पावती (receipt) — the words you need for your first day, your payslip and your resignation.

    Common mistakes

    ✗ Using शिपाई for soldier→✓ शिपाई = peon/attendant; सैनिक = soldier

    Hindi सिपाही = soldier; Marathi शिपाई is the office attendant. The soldier is सैनिक — a direct false friend.

    ✗ Confusing काम (work) and नोकरी (job)→✓ काम = work (activity); नोकरी = job (position)

    मला काम आहे (I have work to do) vs माझी नोकरी आहे (I have a job). Hindi काम/नौकरी follows the same split.

    ✗ Calling a barber न्हावी in formal writing→✓ नाई is the formal term; न्हावी is colloquial

    Both exist; नाई appears in official contexts.

    Quick practice

    Answer first, then open the reveal to check yourself.

    1. What is “salary” in Marathi?
    1. काम
    2. पगार
    3. सुट्टी
    4. बिल

    Answer: B. पगार — पगार (pagār) — same root as Hindi वेतन/पगार.

    2. A tailor is…
    1. सुतार
    2. लोहार
    3. शिंपी
    4. धोबी

    Answer: C. शिंपी — शिंपी (śimpī) = tailor; the surname Shimpī comes from this.

    3. बैठक means…
    1. Salary
    2. Leave
    3. Meeting
    4. Office

    Answer: C. Meeting — बैठक (baiṭhak) = meeting; from बैठणे (to sit).

    4. Fill in the blank: उद्या माझी ___ आहे, तयार राहा.

    मीटिंग — English loanword used daily in offices.
