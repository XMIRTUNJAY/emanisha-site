    What you will learn

    Forty-seven Marathi nature words from the Bol Marathi curriculum: trees (झाड, वड, पिंपळ), flowers (फूल, गुलाब, मोगरा), landforms (शिखर, दरी, बेट, वाळवंट), water bodies (तलाव, धबधबा, समुद्रकिनारा), and the sky above (सूर्य, चंद्र, तारा, आकाश). Every entry has Hindi and English meanings, pronunciation and a real example sentence.

    Hindi speakers will recognize many Sanskrit-rooted words (पर्यावरण, प्रदूषण, हिम) but should note the soft shifts: पेड़ → झाड, तालाब → तलाव, चोटी → शिखर. The table below pairs each so you learn the difference once.

    Tip: Learn in habitat layers: ground (माती, दगड, वाळू), water (तलाव, धबधबा, विहीर), sky (सूर्य, चंद्र, तारा) — the curriculum sentences in Unit 14 follow exactly this layering.

    Sacred trees and festival flowers

    Maharashtra's landscape is marked by trees with cultural weight: वड (banyan), पिंपळ (peepal), तुळस (holy basil), मोगरा (jasmine), झेंडू (marigold). You will hear these at weddings, festivals and daily rituals — they are not decorative vocabulary, they are cultural landmarks.

    For Hindi speakers: soft shifts, not free passes

    Words like हिम (ice/snow), पर्यावरण (environment), प्रदूषण (pollution) are shared. But पेड़ becomes झाड, तालाब becomes तलाव, बीज becomes बी. Learn the shifted forms; the shared ones will reinforce themselves.

    Common mistakes

    ✗ Writing पान for water→✓ पान = leaf; पाणी = water

    The classic false friend — Hindi पान (betel leaf) and पानी (water) sound close; Marathi separates them cleanly: पान (leaf), पाणी (water).

    ✗ Using दरी for door→✓ दरी = valley; दरवाजा = door

    Hindi दरवाजा shortens to दरी in casual speech; Marathi keeps the full word for door and uses दरी only for valley.

    ✗ Saying वाळू for sand everywhere→✓ वाळू = sand; रेत is also used in formal contexts

    वाळू is the everyday word; रेत appears in writing. Both valid; वाळू is what you will hear on a beach.

    1. How do you say “waterfall” in Marathi?
    1. तलाव
    2. धबधबा
    3. विहीर
    4. झरा

    Answer: B. धबधबा — धबधबा (dhabdhabā) — onomatopoeic, like the sound of falling water.

    2. What is आंब्याचं झाड?
    1. Neem tree
    2. Mango tree
    3. Banyan tree
    4. Peepal tree

    Answer: B. Mango tree — आंब्याचं झाड = mango tree (आंबा = mango + झाड = tree).

    3. रेगिस्तान in Marathi is…
    1. वाळवंट
    2. जंगल
    3. दरी
    4. शिखर

    Answer: A. वाळवंट — वाळवंट (vāḷvaṇṭ) — literally “sand-forest”, the desert.

    4. Fill in the blank: सह्याद्रीमध्ये खूप ___ आहेत.

    झाड — The Sahyadris are forested — use the tree word.
