    What you will learn

    Thirty-two Marathi connectors and core grammar words: conjunctions (आणि, पण, कारण, म्हणून, जर, तर, किंवा, आधी, नंतर), demonstratives (हा, ही, हे, इथे, तिथे), pronouns (तो, ती, ते, कोण, कशा) and quantifiers (काही, सर्व, प्रत्येक). Every entry has Hindi and English meanings, pronunciation and a real example sentence.

    These are the mortar between vocabulary bricks — Unit 4 sentence patterns and Unit 21 advanced grammar both build on them. The possessives page (माझा/माझी) shares the demonstrative roots.

    Tip: जर…तर = if…then pair — always appears together in conditional sentences. Memorize the pair, not the parts.

    The conditional pair

    जर पाऊस आला, तर मी घरी राहीन (if rain comes, then I stay home). जर introduces the condition, तर the consequence — drop either and the logic breaks.

    Demonstratives you already know

    हा/ही/हे (this), तो/ती/ते (that), इथे/तिथे (here/there) — the near/far and gender system mirrors possessives (माझा/माझी/माझे, तो/ती/ते). Learn one set, the other follows.

    Common mistakes

    ✗ Using परंतु for everyday “but”→✓ पण = but (everyday); परंतु = but (formal/written)

    Spoken Marathi uses पण exclusively; परंतु appears in official letters and literature.

    ✗ Dropping तर in जर…तर→✓ जर without तर sounds incomplete

    The curriculum treats them as a fixed pair — Unit 4 conditional sentences always show both.

    ✗ Listing with आणि only→✓ आणि is correct for lists; commas work too

    आणि is the standard coordinator; no mistake, just noting it is the only “and” you need.

    Quick practice

    Answer first, then open the reveal to check yourself.

    1. How do you say “but” in everyday Marathi?
    1. परंतु
    2. पण
    3. कारण
    4. म्हणून

    Answer: B. पण — पण (paṇ) — the everyday but; परंतु is formal.

    2. म्हणून means…
    1. Because
    2. Therefore
    3. If
    4. Or

    Answer: B. Therefore — म्हणून (mhaṇūn) = therefore / so; कारण is because.

    3. The pair for “if…then” is…
    1. जर…पण
    2. जर…तर
    3. जर…कारण
    4. तर…जर

    Answer: B. जर…तर — जर…तर (jar…tar) — fixed conditional pair.

    4. Translate to Marathi: “Ram and Lakshman.”

    राम आणि लक्ष्मण. — Unit 21 compound word — the classic 쌍.

    Common questions

    What are the most common Marathi conjunctions?

    आणि (and), पण (but), कारण (because), म्हणून (therefore), जर…तर (if…then) — the five that carry most sentences.

    How do you say “or” in Marathi?

    किंवा (kīṃvā) — same as Hindi कि or या.
