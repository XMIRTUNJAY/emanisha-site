    What you will learn

    Forty-six Marathi phrases from the Phrases category (46) and Adverbs (30) covering feelings (मला आवडतं, मला भीती वाटते), adverbs (खरंच, नक्की, कदाचित, जवळजवळ), and ready-to-use daily expressions (काही हरकत नाही, बरोबर, चूक, पुन्हा सांगा, हळू बोला). Every entry has Hindi and English meanings, pronunciation and a real example sentence.

    These are the phrases you hear and say every day — not grammar exercises, but the actual sentences Marathi speakers use. The Phrases category (46) and Adverbs category (30) overlap here to give you the most useful daily expressions in one place.

    Tip: मला … आवडतं for likes, मला … वाटते for feelings, खरंच/नक्की for emphasis, कदाचित for uncertainty. These four frames cover 80% of daily expressive sentences.

    The feeling frames

    मला … आवडतं (I like …), मला … वाटते (I feel …), मला … आहे (I have … — for hunger, thirst, fever). The dative मला marks the experiencer; the feeling is the subject. Hindi मुझे … है maps directly.

    Emphasis & uncertainty adverbs

    खरंच (really/truly), नक्की (surely/definitely), कदाचित (maybe/perhaps), जवळजवळ (almost). These sit right before the verb or clause they modify: खरंच चांगले आहे (it's really good), कदाचित तो येईल (maybe he will come).

    Ready daily phrases

    काही हरकत नाही (it's alright / no problem), बरोबर (correct/okay), चूक (wrong/mistake), पुन्हा सांगा (say again), हळू बोला (speak slowly), मला समजलं नाही (I didn't understand). These are the social lubricant of Marathi conversation.

    Common mistakes

    ✗ Using खरंच for "really" in all contexts→✓ खरंच = truly/really; नक्की = surely/definitely; use based on certainty level

    खरंच = really/truly (emphasizing truth); नक्की = surely/definitely (expressing certainty). They are not fully interchangeable.

    ✗ Saying कधीच for "sometimes"→✓ कधीकधी = sometimes; कधीच = never

    The च particle negates: कधी (when) → कधीच (never). कधीकधी = sometimes is the positive form.

    ✗ Placing बरोबर at sentence end→✓ बरोबर as agreement comes mid-sentence or as response; as adjective it agrees: बरोबर उत्तर (correct answer)

    बरोबर functions as both interjection ("Right!") and adjective ("correct"). Position depends on function.
