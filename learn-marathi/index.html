    FAQ

    Common questions

    Can I learn Marathi from Hindi?

    Yes — start with Hindi → Marathi words and sentence bridges, which map each Hindi pattern onto Marathi word by word.

    Can I learn Marathi from English?

    Yes — every word and lesson carries English meanings, and there is a dedicated English → Marathi section.

    Is Bol Marathi free?

    All 1,167 words and 31 unit lessons on this site are free. App pricing will be announced at launch.

    How many lessons are there?

    31 units and 186 lessons, from Marathi Fundamentals to a final assessment.

    Does Bol Marathi teach speaking?

    The website teaches reading knowledge; the app adds listening, speaking checks, sentence building and spaced repetition.

    How is the app different from this website?

    The website gives you the knowledge. The app gives you the practice loop — listen, speak, build, review, remember — plus streaks, XP and weakest-word recommendations.
