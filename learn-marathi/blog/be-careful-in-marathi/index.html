    Common Mistakes

    • ❌ ध्यान रखना (Hindi) → ✅ सावध राहा (the Marathi be-careful)
    • ❌ सावधान राहा (heavy) → ✅ सावध राहा is the natural everyday form
    Tip: सावध! shouted once is the everyday 'watch out!' — you will hear it at railway crossings, markets and from every careful grandmother.

    FAQs

    How do you say be careful in Marathi?

    सावध राहा (sāvadh rāhā) — polite. A quick warning is simply सावध! ('watch out!')

    What does सावध mean?

    Alert/careful — from Sanskrit स + वध? No — it means attentive. सावधान is the longer ceremonial form.

    How do you warn someone on the road in Marathi?

    रस्त्यावर सावध राहा or just a sharp सावध! — both are instantly understood.
