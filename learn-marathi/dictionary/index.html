    Marathi Dictionary App

    English to Marathi Dictionary

    Type a word in English or Hindi — get the Marathi word instantly. 1167 curriculum words, every one with pronunciation, Hindi meaning and a real example sentence. Works offline once loaded.

    Tip: the 🔊 button reads each word aloud in Marathi. Example sentences come from the real Bol Marathi curriculum.

    How it works

    Every word comes from the Bol Marathi curriculum — the same 1,167 words used across thevocabulary lists,lessons andgames. Search matches English meanings, Hindi meanings, Devanagari spellings and romanised pronunciation, so however you think of the word, you find it.

    • English to Marathi words — grouped by topic
