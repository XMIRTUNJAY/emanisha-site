    What you will learn

    Six complete market sentences from Unit 09: asking the price, naming a quantity, bargaining politely, accepting, paying online, and asking for the bill. Each maps the English to the Marathi with a speaking tip.

    The frame is identical to Hindi: quantity first (एक किलो), then the verb (द्या). Bargaining is a single line: थोडं कमी करा, कृपया.

    Tip: थोडं कमी करा, कृपया -- say it with a smile. Vendors expect it; it is polite, not rude.

    English → Marathi, sentence by sentence

    How much is this? Fifty rupees.
    ↓
    हे किती आहे? पन्नास रुपये.
    यह कितने का है? पचास रुपये।
    💡किती = how much. रुपये = rupees.
    Give one kilo vegetables.
    ↓
    एक किलो भाजी द्या.
    एक किलो सब्जी दो।
    💡किलो = kilo. द्या = give.
    Reduce a little, please.
    ↓
    थोडं कमी करा, कृपया.
    थोड़ा कम करो, कृपया।
    💡कमी = less. कृपया = please.
    This is good, I'll take it.
    ↓
    हे छान आहे, मी घेईन.
    यह अच्छा है, मैं लूँगा।
    💡छान = good. घेईन = will take.
    I'll pay online.
    ↓
    मी ऑनलाइन देनार.
    मैं ऑनलाइन दूँगा।
    💡ऑनलाइन = online. देनार = will give.
    Give receipt, please.
    ↓
    बिल द्या, कृपया.
    बिल दो, कृपया।
    💡बिल = bill/receipt.

    The quantity + verb frame

    English "Give me one kilo" → Marathi एक किलो भाजी द्या -- quantity (एक किलो) + noun (भाजी) + verb (द्या). The noun changes, the frame stays.

    Bargaining & payment

    थोडं कमी करा, कृपया (reduce a little please) -- the standard polite bargain. मी ऑनलाइन देनार (I'll pay online) and बिल द्या, कृपया (give the bill please) close the transaction.

    Common mistakes

    ✗ Using कितना for price→✓ किती = how much

    Hindi कितना/कितनी/कितने collapses to Marathi किती -- one word for all.

    ✗ Saying महँगा for expensive→✓ महाग -- nasal dropped

    Marathi drops the nasal: महँगा → महाग, सस्ता → स्वस्त.

    ✗ Saying नकद for cash→✓ रोख = cash

    नकद is formal; रोख is the everyday Marathi word.

    Quick practice

    Answer first, then open the reveal to check yourself.

    1. How much is this?
    1. हे कितना आहे?
    2. हे किती आहे?
    3. हे कितने आहे?
    4. किती हे आहे?

    Answer: B. हे किती आहे? — हे किती आहे? -- the standard price question.

    2. Reduce a little, please.
    1. थोडा कम करो
    2. थोडं कमी करा, कृपया
    3. कमी करा
    4. थोडं करा

    Answer: B. थोडं कमी करा, कृपया — थोडं कमी करा, कृपया -- exact Unit 09 line.

    3. Cash in Marathi?
    1. नकद
    2. रोख
    3. पैसा
    4. पावती

    Answer: B. रोख — रोख (rokh) -- everyday word; नकद is formal.

    4. Fill in the blank: एक किलो _____ द्या.

    भाजी — Generic vegetable -- Unit 09 market sentence.

    Common questions

    How do you bargain in Marathi?

    थोडं कमी करा, कृपया -- reduce a little, please. The polite कृपया makes it standard etiquette.

    What is the word for "expensive"?

    महाग -- same root as महँगा, nasal dropped.
