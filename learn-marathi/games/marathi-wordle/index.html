    Daily Marathi Wordle

    Marathi Wordle

    Guess the Marathi word in six tries. Every tile is one akshara — a full syllable, never a broken letter.

    How to play
    1. Each guess must be the right length — build it by tapping the Marathi keyboard.
    2. After you submit, tiles change color: green = right akshara, right spot; amber = right akshara, wrong spot; grey = not in the word.
    3. Tap ⏎ (or press Enter) to submit and ⌫ (Backspace) to delete.
    4. A new word arrives every day (UTC). Practice mode gives unlimited words from the same pool.
    5. Wordle is tough — tap 💡 Hint for the meaning, then the first akshara.
