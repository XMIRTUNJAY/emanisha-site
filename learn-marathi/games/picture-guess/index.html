    Marathi Picture Guess

    Picture Guess

    A picture, four words — which one is it? Words come from the curriculum and every picture is an original illustration made for this site.

    How to play
    1. Pick a level: easy words are everyday objects, challenge words are longer or rarer.
    2. Not sure? Open a hint: the meaning first, then the pronunciation, then the first letter.
    3. After answering you get the Hindi meaning, an example sentence and audio.
