## What is a page, really?

A **search intent** is the job a searcher wants done: "marathi food words" wants a browsable list, "how to say sorry in Marathi" wants one phrase with pronunciation, "marathi verbs" wants a reference table. A **page** on this site is one intent, fully answered. That single rule — one intent, one page, fully answered — decided all 200 URLs. Everything in this part is that rule applied to real counts.

## The inventory: what the curriculum holds

The synced snapshot (`src/data/learn/meta.json`, stamped 2026-09-20) counts 26 units, 156 lessons, and 1,002 words across 29 categories. The big categories read like page titles waiting to happen:

| -------- | ------- | ------ |
| Verbs | 105 | Vocabulary cluster |
| Food | 84 | Vocabulary cluster |
| Home | 65 | Part of daily-life cluster |
| Travel | 50 | Travel cluster |
| Directions | 16 | Merged into travel |
| Family | 15 | Family cluster |
| Shopping | 12 | Merged (with numbers/scripts) |
| People | 10 | Merged into family |
| Pronouns | 8 | Merged into family/grammar |

The pattern: above roughly 15 entries, a category stands alone. Below it, the content joins a neighbor instead of getting its own page. That number is the **size gate**, and it is the cheapest important decision on the site.

## What didn't become pages (and why)

Three rejections did more for the site's quality than any page it shipped:

- **Per-word pages (1,002 candidates → zero pages).** A single word with one example is the textbook definition of thin content. Words ship inside clusters and topic pages that clear the validator's minimums instead.
- **Per-lesson pages (156 candidates → 26 unit pages).** One lesson row alone can't hold a page; six sections bundled per unit can. Fewer pages, denser pages.
- **Micro-clusters (Shopping 12, Colors 13, People 10, Pronouns 8, Routine 7 → merged).** Each joined a sibling — shopping with numbers and market scripts, people and pronouns with family — instead of standing alone.

<Callout type="lesson" title="Habit worth copying">
  Count before you create the page. List the entries the page would contain; if the list is short, the content belongs on an existing page. The gate number (~15 here) matters less than having one and enforcing it.
</Callout>

## The alternative it beat

The alternative was hand-written Markdown, one file per page, maintained by hand. At 200 pages that means 200 files drifting from the app's curriculum with every app update — new words never appearing, fixed translations never propagating. The dataset engine in Part 3 exists so the route table above is generated from data that itself is synced from data. The intent map decides *what* pages exist; the engine decides *how* they stay true.
